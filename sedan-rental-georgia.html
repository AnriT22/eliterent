Sedan Rental FAQ

Sedan or SUV in Georgia?+

Pick a sedan for cities, airports and paved highways. Choose an SUV or 4x4 only for mountain or unpaved roads.

Good for airport transfers?+

Yes — a mid-size sedan seats four with luggage and is popular for Tbilisi airport pickups.
