Kutaisi Car Rental FAQ

Everything you need to know

How much does it cost to rent a car in Kutaisi?

From $22/day for economy cars. Kutaisi tends to be cheaper than Tbilisi or Batumi.

Can I pick up a car at Kutaisi Airport?

Yes! Partners offer pickup at David the Builder Kutaisi International Airport — popular with Wizz Air passengers.

How far is Kutaisi from Tbilisi?

About 230km, 2.5-3 hours by car via the highway. One-way rentals between cities are available.

What can I visit from Kutaisi by car?

Prometheus Cave (30min), Martvili Canyon (1h), Okatse Canyon (45min), Gelati Monastery (15min), Sataplia Nature Reserve (15min), Tskaltubo (20min). All best explored with your own car.
