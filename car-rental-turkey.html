Turkey car rental FAQ

Do I need an International Driving Permit in Turkey?+

Most visitors can drive on their national licence for up to six months, but rules vary by nationality and some partners ask for an IDP alongside a non-Latin-script licence. Carry your passport and licence, and check the requirement for your country before you travel.

How old do I have to be to rent a car in Turkey?+

Most partners require drivers to be at least 21 with a licence held for one to two years. Larger and premium vehicles often set a higher minimum age. The exact requirement is shown on each listing.

Is a deposit required?+

It depends on the partner. Some hold a refundable deposit on a credit card, others do not. Each listing states the deposit before you book, so you can pick the terms that suit you.

Can I drive a Turkish rental car into another country?+

Usually not. Most Turkish rental agreements do not allow crossing borders, including into Georgia. If you need a cross-border trip, confirm it in writing with the partner first.

Are tolls automatic on Turkish motorways?+

Yes. Turkish motorways and the Istanbul bridges use the electronic HGS system. Ask your partner how tolls are handled on their vehicle — most cars carry a transponder and the partner bills tolls afterwards.
