Istanbul Airport car rental FAQ

Where do I meet the car at Istanbul Airport?+

Most partners meet you in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name, then walk you to the car park. Exact instructions are sent before your flight lands.

What if my flight is delayed?+

Give your flight number when booking. Partners track arrivals and adjust to delays — that is why the flight number is requested rather than just a pickup time.

Can I return the car at Sabiha Gokcen instead?+

Sometimes, for a relocation fee, since the airports are on opposite sides of the city. Confirm it with the partner before booking rather than assuming.

How far is Istanbul Airport from the city centre?+

About 40 km to Sultanahmet — typically 45 to 75 minutes depending on traffic, which in Istanbul varies a great deal by time of day.
