Deposit vs. No-Deposit — What to Expect

Every partner sets their own policy. EliteAuto.rent shows the deposit amount (or “No deposit”) on each listing so you can compare before booking.

No deposit

Pay only the rental fee and optional insurance. Common for economy and mid-range cars with trusted partners.

Low deposit ($200–$500)

Refundable hold released after the car is returned in agreed condition.

Luxury / 4x4

Higher-value vehicles may require a deposit or full CDW — always shown upfront.
