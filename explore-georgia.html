Top Regions to Explore

Kakheti

Georgia's wine country: family marani cellars, qvevri winemaking and rolling vineyards in the Alazani Valley.

Sighnaghi · Telavi · Alaverdi Monastery · Bodbe
Learn more →
Kazbegi

The Georgian Military Highway to Stepantsminda and the iconic Gergeti Trinity Church beneath Mount Kazbek. In winter, the same road leads to Gudauri ski resort.

Gergeti Trinity · Gudauri · Ananuri · Jvari Pass
Learn more →
Svaneti

Remote high-mountain villages with medieval Svan towers, glaciers and the UNESCO village of Ushguli.

Mestia · Ushguli · Svan towers · Chalaadi Glacier
Learn more →
Adjara & Batumi

The Black Sea coast: Batumi's seaside boulevard and modern skyline, plus green Adjaran mountains and waterfalls.

Batumi Boulevard · Gonio · Mtirala Park · Makhuntseti
Learn more →
Tbilisi

The capital's old town: sulphur baths, cobbled streets, the cable car to Narikala Fortress and lively wine bars.

Old Town · Narikala · Abanotubani · Rustaveli Ave
Learn more →
Mtskheta

Georgia's ancient royal capital and spiritual heart — a UNESCO site just 20 minutes from Tbilisi.

Svetitskhoveli · Jvari Monastery · Samtavro
Learn more →
