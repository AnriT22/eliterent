Cheap Car Rental FAQ

How much does it cost to rent a car in Georgia?+

From about $25/day for economy, $45–70/day for SUVs and $80+/day for luxury. Longer bookings and the low season are cheapest. See live prices.

How do I rent a car cheaply?+

Book early, choose economy, rent for a week or more, travel off-peak, and pick a no-deposit listing.

Are there hidden fees?+

No. Basic insurance is included on most cars and every optional fee or deposit is shown clearly before you book.
