Economy Rental FAQ

What is the cheapest car to rent in Georgia?+

Economy hatchbacks like the Hyundai Accent, Kia Rio and Toyota Yaris start around $25/day — cheapest for longer bookings and low season. Browse all cars.

Automatic or manual?+

Both are available; manuals are often a little cheaper. The gearbox is listed on every vehicle.

Is an economy car good for a road trip?+

Great for cities and paved highways like Tbilisi–Batumi. For mountains (Kazbegi, Svaneti), choose an SUV or 4x4.
