Antalya Airport car rental FAQ

Where do I collect the car at Antalya Airport?+

Partners meet you in the arrivals hall with a name sign and walk you to the vehicle. You receive the meeting point and a contact number before departure.

How far is Antalya Airport from the resorts?+

Roughly 15 minutes to central Antalya, an hour to Side, around two hours to Alanya and three to Kaş along the coastal road.

Is an automatic car available at AYT?+

Yes, though automatics are in high demand over summer and sell out first. Filter for automatic transmission and book early if you need one.

Do I need snow chains for the mountain roads?+

Only in winter, and only if you head inland over the Taurus passes toward Isparta or Cappadocia. The coast itself stays mild.
