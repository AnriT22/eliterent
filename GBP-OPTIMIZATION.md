## 10. SEED Q&A (Post These Yourself, Then Let Customers Add More)

**Q1:** Do you offer airport pickup at Tbilisi Airport?
**A:** Yes, we offer free meet-and-greet pickup at Tbilisi International Airport (TBS). Our representative waits at the arrivals hall with a sign showing your name. The same service is available at Kutaisi and Batumi Airports.

**Q2:** Can I rent a car without a deposit?
**A:** Yes, selected cars in our fleet are available with no deposit required. These are marked as "No deposit" on our website. You only need your passport and driving license.

**Q3:** What driving license do I need?
**A:** Most international visitors need an International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside their home license. Some national licenses (EU, USA, UK) are accepted directly. Contact us before your trip to confirm.

**Q4:** Is insurance included?
**A:** Basic third-party insurance is included in all rentals. Full insurance with zero excess is available as an affordable add-on during booking.

**Q5:** Are automatic cars available?
**A:** Yes, we have automatic sedans, SUVs, and minivans. Each car listing clearly shows transmission type. We recommend booking automatic cars in advance as they are in high demand.

**Q6:** Do you have cars with winter tires?
**A:** Yes, from November through March our mountain fleet is equipped with winter tires. This is essential for safe driving to Kazbegi, Svaneti, and other mountain regions.

**Q7:** Can I pick up in Tbilisi and drop off in Batumi?
**A:** Yes, one-way rentals are available between Tbilisi, Batumi, and Kutaisi. A one-way fee applies and is shown clearly before you confirm your booking.

**Q8:** What is the minimum age to rent?
**A:** The minimum age is 21 years old with at least 2 years of driving experience. Drivers under 25 may be subject to a young driver surcharge on some vehicles.

**Q9:** Do you offer child seats?
**A:** Yes, ISOFIX child seats for infants and toddlers are available free of charge. Please request one when booking so we can have it installed before pickup.

**Q10:** Is roadside assistance included?
**A:** Yes, 24/7 roadside assistance is included with every rental. Call or WhatsApp us at +995 591 522 299 anytime if you need help.

## 11. REVIEW GENERATION STRATEGY

### How to Ask (Do This Every Time)
1. **After successful return:** Send WhatsApp message: "Hi [Name], hope you enjoyed Georgia! If you have 30 seconds, a quick Google review would mean the world to us and helps other travelers. [link]"
2. **At airport pickup:** Hand customer a small card with QR code linking to leave-a-review.html
3. **Email follow-up:** 24 hours after return, send email with subject "How was your trip?" including the review link
4. **Incentive:** Offer 5% off next rental for leaving a review (not "for a 5-star review" — that violates Google policy)

### Rules
- Never buy fake reviews
- Never offer money for reviews
- Never ask for "5 stars" specifically — ask for "honest feedback"
- Reply to 100% of reviews within 24 hours
- Target 2–4 new reviews per month (quality over quantity)
