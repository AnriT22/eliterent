Batumi Airport Rental FAQ

Where do I meet the agent at BUS?+

Most partners meet you in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name. Because the terminal is small, handover is fast — exact instructions are sent before you land.

Can I drive across the border into Turkey?+

The Sarpi (Turkey) border is about 20 km south, but most Georgian rental agreements do not permit crossing into Turkey. If you need a cross-border trip, confirm it in writing with the partner before booking.

Can I pick up in Batumi and return in Tbilisi?+

Yes — one-way rentals between Batumi and Tbilisi or Kutaisi are often available for a relocation fee. Check the partner's terms on your chosen listing.
