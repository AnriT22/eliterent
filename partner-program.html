To list a car we need the basics. If something is missing we will ask — nothing goes live half-finished.

The car

Brand, model, year, transmission, fuel, seats and clear photos.

The terms

Daily price, deposit, mileage limit, insurance and minimum driver age.

The logistics

Where the car can be collected, whether you deliver to airports, and who to contact for bookings.

Partner FAQ

What does it cost to join?+

For rental companies it is free — we issue you an invite code, so there is no signup fee and no monthly cost. Our service fee is added to the customer's booking, which means the rate you set is the rate you receive.

Do I lose control of my prices?+

No. You tell us the rate you want for each car. We agree the final listed price with you before publishing.

What if a car is already rented out?+

Tell us and we block those dates, or we mark the car as needing confirmation before each booking. Customers can cancel free of charge within 24 hours, so a clash is never a disaster.

Do I need a website or booking system?+

No. Most of our partners do not have one. Send us your car list in any format and we will handle the rest.

Can I remove my cars later?+

Yes. You can pause or remove any car at any time, and there is no long-term lock-in.
