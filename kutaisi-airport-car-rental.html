Why Rent at Kutaisi Airport?

✈

Budget-Flight Friendly

KUT is Georgia's low-cost gateway. Partners are used to late-night Wizz Air and Ryanair arrivals and offer true 24/7 handover.

🏖

Gateway to the Black Sea

Drive to Batumi and the coast in about 2 hours, or reach Tbilisi in roughly 3.5 hours — on your schedule, not a bus timetable.

Limited Transit — Car Recommended

Public transport from the airport is sparse. A rental is usually cheaper than transfers for two or more travellers.

Kutaisi Airport Rental FAQ

Where do I meet the agent at KUT?+

Most partners meet you in the compact arrivals hall holding a sign with your name. Exact instructions are sent before your flight lands.

Can I rent at KUT and return in Tbilisi or Batumi?+

Yes — one-way rentals from Kutaisi Airport to Tbilisi or Batumi are often available for a small relocation fee. Check the partner's terms on your chosen listing.

My flight lands at night — is that a problem?+

No. The majority of KUT flights are off-hours, so partners handle late-night and early-morning handovers as standard at no extra charge on most listings.
