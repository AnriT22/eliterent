Monthly Rental FAQ

How much is monthly car rental in Tbilisi?+

From around $450/month for economy, more for SUVs and larger cars. The longer the term, the lower the daily rate. See available cars.

Is it cheaper than renting daily?+

Yes — usually 30–40% cheaper per day. For stays of three weeks or more, monthly is almost always the best value. Compare with budget daily rates.

What's included?+

Basic insurance, routine maintenance and a monthly mileage allowance on most listings; free Tbilisi delivery is common. Exact terms are shown per car.

Can I extend?+

Yes — extend month to month or switch vehicles by arrangement. Tell us your dates and we'll match you to a long-term listing.
