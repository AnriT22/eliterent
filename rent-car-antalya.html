Antalya car rental FAQ

How far is Cappadocia from Antalya by car?+

Roughly 500 km, about six to seven hours each way depending on the route. Most travellers break the drive with a night in Konya or Isparta rather than doing it in one push.

Is the coastal road from Antalya difficult to drive?+

The D400 coastal highway is well surfaced and clearly signed, but it is winding with steep sections between Antalya and Kaş. Allow more time than the distance suggests and avoid rushing it after dark.

Do I need an SUV in Antalya?+

Not for the coast or main routes — an economy or sedan is fine. An SUV is worth considering if you plan mountain roads inland or are travelling with a full car of luggage.

When is the best time to drive the Turkish coast?+

May, June, September and October give warm weather without peak-summer traffic. July and August are hot and busy, and coastal parking fills early.
