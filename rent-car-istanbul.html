Istanbul car rental FAQ

Which Istanbul airport should I pick up from?+

Choose the airport your flight actually lands at — they are on opposite sides of the city and roughly 90 minutes apart. Istanbul Airport (IST) serves most long-haul flights; Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) handles many budget and domestic routes.

Is driving in Istanbul difficult?+

Traffic is dense and drivers are assertive, but the roads are good and signposting is clear. Many visitors collect the car on the way out of the city rather than driving in the centre, and use public transport downtown.

Do I need to pay for the Bosphorus bridges?+

Yes, the bridges and motorways are tolled electronically through the HGS system. Most rental cars carry a transponder — ask your partner how they bill tolls after the rental.

Where can I park in Istanbul?+

Street parking in the historic centre is very limited. Most hotels offer paid parking, and municipal İSPARK car parks are widespread and reasonably priced.
