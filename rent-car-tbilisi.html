Tbilisi Car Rental FAQ

Everything you need to know

How much does it cost to rent a car in Tbilisi?

Car rental in Tbilisi starts from $25/day for economy cars, $40/day for SUVs, and $80+/day for luxury vehicles. Prices vary by season — summer (June-September) is peak season.

Can I pick up a rental car at Tbilisi Airport?

Yes! EliteAuto.rent partners offer 24/7 airport pickup and delivery at Tbilisi International Airport (TBS). Your car will be waiting when you land — no extra fees.

Do I need a deposit to rent a car in Tbilisi?

It depends on the partner. Many of our Tbilisi partners offer no-deposit rentals. Browse available cars and filter by deposit-free options.

What documents do I need to rent a car in Tbilisi?

You need a valid driver's license (international or from your home country), passport, and a credit/debit card. Minimum age is typically 21.

Where can I drive from Tbilisi?

Popular routes: Tbilisi → Kazbegi (3h), Tbilisi → Kakheti wine region (1.5h), Tbilisi → Gudauri (2h), Tbilisi → Batumi (5h). An SUV is recommended for mountain roads.
