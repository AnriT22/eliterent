Batumi Car Rental FAQ

Everything you need to know

How much does it cost to rent a car in Batumi?

From $25/day for economy, $35/day for crossovers, $50+/day for SUVs. Summer (June-Sept) is peak season with higher prices.

Can I pick up a car at Batumi Airport?

Yes! Our partners offer airport pickup at Batumi International Airport. Available 24/7 during peak season.

Can I drive from Batumi to Tbilisi?

Yes, the drive is 5-6 hours via the main highway. Many travelers do one-way rentals — ask the partner about drop-off options.

Best drives from Batumi?

Batumi → Gonio Fortress (20min), Sarpi Turkish border (30min), Kintrishi National Park (1h), Machakhela gorge, and the coastal road to Kobuleti & Ureki magnetic beach.
